## Neon Manifesto

This post exists to test the custom CSS system. The words are secondary to the visual experience — a deliberate exercise in letting the container speak as loudly as the content.

## What Custom CSS Enables

Every post on this platform can carry its own stylesheet. Not merely a theme — a full visual identity. The author is not just a writer. They are an art director.

This changes the relationship between content and form. The design is not a neutral frame around the words. It is part of the argument.

## The Electric Case

There is a tradition of design that refuses to be background — that insists on being noticed, that treats every pixel as intentional. Neon is that tradition made literal. Visible in the dark. Impossible to ignore.

Some ideas deserve to glow.

## Conclusion

If you are reading this in its intended styling, the text appears against a dark background with electric accent colours. The heading glows faintly. The blockquotes pulse with colour.

If you are reading this in plain text, imagine all of the above and consider it done.
