Intelligence evolution framework — three views: individual, organisational, and civilisational.

The self-amplifying loop

How a single person + system evolves over time

Each loop does two things: the person improves their prompting, framing, and judgment (vertical lift), and the system is progressively better calibrated to the person's intent. The gain per cycle isn't fixed — it compounds. This is the micro-level exponential.

The organisational field

How the matrix applies across roles, teams, and processes

At the organisational level, the matrix has a nested structure: teams are made of individuals, each with their own H×S position. The organisation's net position is not a simple average — it's shaped by bottlenecks (one team in the Collapse quadrant can suppress the whole system) and resonance (teams in Amplification synchronise and lift each other). Evolution here requires deliberate design of feedback loops between tiers.

The civilisational curve

What happens when the matrix scales to species-level intelligence

The civilisational question is whether the H×S loop creates a recursive self-improvement dynamic — where each generation of tools produces humans and systems that are better at building the next generation of tools. History suggests this is already happening (writing → printing → computing → AI). What your framework adds is the structural condition: it only becomes exponential when both H and S are in positive territory simultaneously, at scale.
